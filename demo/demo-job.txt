Job Title: Full Stack Developer Intern

Position: Full Stack Developer Intern
Job Type: Internship
Work Mode: Remote/Hybrid/On-site
Duration: 3–6 Months

Job Description

We are looking for a motivated Full Stack Developer Intern to join our development team. The ideal candidate should have a basic understanding of frontend and backend development, databases, and REST APIs, along with a willingness to learn and work on real-world projects.

Key Responsibilities

* Develop and maintain responsive web applications.
* Build user-friendly interfaces using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React.js.
* Develop backend services and REST APIs using Node.js and Express.js.
* Work with databases such as MongoDB or MySQL.
* Integrate third-party APIs and external services.
* Debug applications, fix issues, and optimize performance.
* Collaborate with team members using Git and GitHub.
* Participate in code reviews and contribute to project development.

Required Skills

* Basic knowledge of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
* Familiarity with React.js.
* Basic understanding of Node.js and Express.js.
* Knowledge of MongoDB or MySQL.
* Understanding of REST APIs and JSON.
* Familiarity with Git and GitHub.
* Good problem-solving and communication skills.

Preferred Qualifications

* Pursuing a B.Tech, B.E., BCA, MCA, or a related degree.
* Personal projects or previous internship experience in web development.
* Understanding of responsive design and authentication.
* Willingness to learn new technologies and work in a team.

What We Offer

* Hands-on experience with real-world projects.
* Opportunity to work with experienced developers.
* Practical exposure to full-stack development.
* Certificate of completion and potential full-time opportunities based on performance.

How to Apply

Interested candidates can share their updated resume along with links to their GitHub profile or portfolio.
